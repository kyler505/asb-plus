We check meeting-time conflicts within each listed section. Public data may not pair separate lecture and lab sections. Confirm class components, live availability, and eligibility in Howdy.

Ranking uses historical course GPA and A/B share, public open/closed flags, and how well meeting times fit your preferences. These are signals, not predictions. Exact score: 2 × mean GPA + mean A/B percent ÷ 25 + open flags - closed flags - early/late hours outside your preferences. Missing grade values are not scored.
