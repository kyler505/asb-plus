Schedule generation and fit

Generate every conflict-free combination for the selected courses. Compare ranked choices or fit them around classes from your own schedule.

Meeting blocks for one CRN are checked together. The public data does not reliably link separate lecture and lab CRNs, so ASB+ does not guess pairs. Confirm components and eligibility in Howdy. Public open flags are not live seat counts.

Ranking: 2 × mean course GPA + mean course A/B percent ÷ 25 + open flags - closed flags - early/late hours outside your preferences. Missing grade values are not scored. The seat signal is only a public open flag. Ratings and walking distance are not scored because approved ratings and reliable campus paths are not available.
