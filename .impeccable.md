## Design Context

### Users
Kyler uses the extension in his own signed-in TAMU Schedule Builder page while choosing courses for Spring 2027. He needs to compare real section availability and meeting times with historical grades, instructor context, degree needs, and saved-section conflicts without switching tools.

### Brand Personality
Focused, trustworthy, efficient. Favor calm confidence and clear evidence over delight or sales language. Labels distinguish live page observations from backend history and unknown values.

### Aesthetic Direction
TAMU-integrated: compact, legible, and aligned with Schedule Builder's existing page density, with an unmistakable but restrained ASB+ workspace identity. Use a light, high-contrast surface and a limited maroon accent. Avoid a dramatic redesign, gradients, generic dashboard cards, and anything that obscures the native page or its registration controls.

### Design Principles
- Keep section choice, useful context, and conflict feedback together in one workspace.
- Preserve the native Schedule Builder and fail open whenever route or DOM confidence is low.
- Make provenance, term, freshness, and unknown data explicit; never invent numeric seats or eligibility.
- Keep degree rules and schedule ranking local; registration is always a deliberate native action by Kyler.
- Optimize for fast scanning, keyboard use, responsive reflow, and accessible status cues.
